## 📖 Overview
<a id="overview"></a>
TinyCAD is your go-to solution for designing printed circuit boards with ease. This full version of **TinyCAD free download** delivers all features and updates, allowing users to create intricate electronic designs without any hassle. The software boasts an intuitive interface that caters to both novices and seasoned professionals in the electronics field.

Designed specifically to streamline the process of circuit design, TinyCAD is compatible with FreePCB and provides a convenient drawing tool for creating detailed circuit layouts. Users can export their designs in PNG format, making it simple to share projects online or incorporate them into documentation. Plus, with integration options for SPICE, you can easily simulate and verify your circuit’s functionality.
